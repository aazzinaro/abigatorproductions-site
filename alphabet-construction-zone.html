Support & FAQ

Questions, bugs, or ideas? Email andrew@abigatorproductions.com.

What is free, and what does the unlock cost?

Build A Letter, Break The Letter, Starts With Letter and the sticker book are free forever. Rhyme Time, Count the Bricks, Memory Match and Trace Town each let you play one free round every time the app opens. A single one-time purchase, Unlock Everything, opens all of them for good. There are no subscriptions and no ads.

How do I unlock everything?

Open a locked game and tap “For grown-ups”, answer the short grown-ups question, and the unlock page opens. You can also reach it from Settings. Children never see a price or a buy button on their own.

I bought it already but the games are still locked.

Open the unlock page and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with. A purchase can be shared with your family through Apple Family Sharing.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and choose the purchase.

Does my child’s progress get lost?

Progress (which letters are built and stickers earned) is stored on your device only. It stays when you update the app, but deleting the app removes it.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later, in landscape orientation.
